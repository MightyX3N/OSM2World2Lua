# Texture sources

Its configuration and own textures are public domain (CC0); most textures come from [ambientCG](https://ambientcg.com/) (CC0) and the [OSM Wiki Texture Library](https://wiki.openstreetmap.org/wiki/Texture_Library) (public domain).
